CASE STUDY - 01

Emerson Innovation Centre - VRF System Modernisation

Upgrade of a critical innovation facility where HVAC reliability directly impacted operational continuity and engineering performance.

KEY CHALLENGES

  • Obsolete air-conditioning system causing repeated operational failures
  • Rising maintenance overhead and inefficiency across the facility
  • Performace instability impacting engineering activity and uptime
  • Existing system installed by a competitor-using Emerson's own compressors

ENGNEERING APPROACH

  • Detailed technical audit of the existing infrastructure, load patterns and failure history
  • Designed a 1200 HP VRF platform optimised for load adaptability and energy efficiency
  • Scalable system architecture aligned with real operational demand of the innovation facility
  • Zone-level redundancy built in to protect critical R&D activity

EXECUTION STRATEGY

  • Consultative engineering-led engagement, not a vendor-style installation
  • Technical presentation and stakeholder validation of the proposed system
  • Precision execution sequenced to minimise disruption to ongoing R&D operations

RESULTS & PERFORMANCE

  • Commissioned within the planned timeline with no operational interruption
  • Stabilised system performance under continuous usage conditions
  • Reduced dependency on frequent maintenance cycles
  • Improved efficiency aligned with the facility's actual load patterns

STRATEGIC IMPACT

  • Secured 600 HP VRF project for the Emerson Energy Export Centre
  • Awarded the complete HVAC revamp of EIC Pune
  • Established a long-term engineering partnership with Emerson





CASE STUDY - 02

Pune Metro Line 3 - Air Conditioning & Ventilation System

A large-scale public infrastructure programme spanning 23 metro stations, administrative buildigs and supporting facilities-requiring HVAC and ventilation systems engineered for continuous public use.

KEY CHALLENGES

  • Massive execution scale at ~2200 HP combined capacity
  • Multi-location deployment across the metro network
  • Programme duration exceeding two years with evolving site conditions
  • Coordination complexity across multiple contractors and stakeholders
  • Timeline overruns and contractor exits introducing industry-level disruption

ENGNEERING APPROACH

  • Designed HVAC and ventilation systems for continuous public usage and safety compliance
  • Engineering focus on durability and long service life under high-load infrastructure conditions
  • Adaptive system planning to absorb evolving site, structural and programme conditions

EXECUTION STRATEGY

  • Strong resource planning and allocation across active sites
  • Simultaneous execution managed across multiple metro stations
  • Continuous coordination with Tata Projects and the wider stakeholder group
  • Sustained delivery despite contractor exits and external programme pressure

RESULTS & PERFORMANCE

  • Maintained execution continuity within a disrupted project environment
  • Delivered consistent system performance across multiple sites
  • Ensured compliance with infrastructure-grade safety requirements
  • Achieved phased delivery aligned with overall programme progress

STRATEGIC IMPACT

  • Awarded via collaboration with Daikin Airconditioning India Pvt. Ltd.
  • Long-term partnership established with Tata Projects
  • Strengthened SVESPL positioning across government and infrastructure sectors
  • Metro station concourse - HVAC and ventilation integration

CASE STUDY - 03

Amanora Township - VRF for Premium Residential Towers

A premium high-rise residential development - Tower 98 and Tower 99 - requiring large-scale VRF air conditioning aligned precisely with phased occupancy schedules.

KEY CHALLENGES

  • High-rise installation logistics across two active towers
  • Over 2000 HP system requirement with vertical distribution constraints
  • Strict alignment with resident move-in timelines
  • Budget constraints alongside large manpower deployment

ENGNEERING APPROACH

  • VRF system optimised for vertical distribution and multi-unit zoning
  • Architecture designed for phased activation in line with handover sequence
  • Scalable, long-life performance built into the design baseline

EXECUTION STRATEGY

  • Phased deployment mapped to per-tower handover timelines
  • Controlled manpower allocation for cost and time efficiency
  • Continuous coordination with City Corporation Ltd throughout the build

RESULTS & PERFORMANCE

  • Delivered large-scale VRF system across both high-rise towers
  • Maintained alignment with phased occupancy schedules
  • Controlled execution within the defined cost structure
  • Stable, consistent performance across all residential units

STRATEGIC IMPACT

  • Awarded the Ultra Premium Tower 100 project
  • Delivered 1000 TR chilled water system for Roha Spa & Resort
  • Executed City Corporation Head Office HVAC
  • Awarded Avenue Mall - 800 TR with full ventilation systems
  • Established as long-term engineering partner to City Corporation Ltd
